# Eurovision 2026 Voting Tracker — How to Use

## Getting started

1. Download the file **eurovision2026.html**
2. Double-click it — it opens in your browser, no internet needed
3. That's it, you're ready

---

## During the show

### Step 1 — Jury scores

After the jury spokespersons have all announced their 12-point scores, the show
displays the **final jury total** for each country. Enter those totals into the
table (one number per country, press **Enter** to move to the next row).

When all 25 are in, click **Confirm Jury Scores**.

> **Tip:** eurovisionworld.com usually publishes the full jury table within
> minutes of it being displayed on screen — you can copy from there.

---

### Step 2 — Televote countdown

Countries are now listed in reveal order — lowest jury score first, matching
what you see on TV.

As each country's televote total is announced:
1. Type the number into the score box
2. Press **Enter** (or click **Reveal**)

The app updates instantly and shows:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 🏆 | Current leader |
| ✅ | Still in the running |
| ❌ | Mathematically eliminated — can't win even with maximum remaining points |
| ⏳ | Not yet revealed |

**Needs to lead** — the gold/red badge next to each country shows exactly how
many televote points they need to overtake the current leader. Gold = still
possible. Red = out of reach.

**Guaranteed winner** — when only one country remains and the pool of remaining
points is already enough to make them the winner, the 🏆 banner appears before
their number is even read out.

## Testing before tonight

Click **Load 2025 Test Data** (top right) to run through last year's results
from Basel, where Austria's JJ won with 436 points. Use **▶ Auto-reveal next**
to step through one country at a time, or **⏩ Auto-reveal all** to jump to the
final result.

Click **↺ Reset** to wipe the test data and go back to the live 2026 setup.

---

## The leaderboard (right side)

Updates after every reveal. For each country it shows:
- Their **combined total**
- The **jury + televote split** (blue = jury, orange = people's vote)
- How many points they are **behind the leader**
